How to play

  • Click a tube to pick up its top color, then click another tube to pour it in.
  • You can only pour onto an empty tube or one whose top color matches.
  • Sort all four colors into their own tubes before the 2-minute timer runs out.
  • Your opponent's board updates live so you can watch them race.
  • Made a mistake? Use Undo to step back one move.
